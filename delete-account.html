Delete Your AppTesting Account

AppTesting
Last updated: October 29, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your AppTesting account and the personal information associated with it.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to ansul5658@gmail.com from the Google account email address you use to sign in to AppTesting.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my AppTesting account".
  3. We may reply to confirm that the request came from the account owner.
  4. Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days of a verified request, and we will email you once it is complete.

Data That Is Deleted

  • Your AppTesting profile and account identifier.
  • Google account information (name, email address, Google account identifier).
  • Applications you added as a developer.
  • Testing commitments, check-ins, testing progress, and group memberships.
  • Testing Coins balance and wallet transaction history.
  • Device information and push notification tokens.

Data That May Be Retained

  • Ratings, comments, and bug reports you submitted may remain visible to developers in anonymized form that no longer identifies you.
  • Limited records needed for security, fraud and abuse prevention, or dispute resolution may be kept for up to 90 days after deletion, or longer only where required by law.

Before You Delete

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Any Testing Coins in your wallet, including coins locked in active testing commitments, will be forfeited, and you will be removed from any testing commitments you have joined.

You can also request deletion of only some of your data without deleting your account by emailing ansul5658@gmail.com with details of the data you want removed.
