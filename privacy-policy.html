Privacy Policy

AppTesting
Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AppTesting collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the AppTesting mobile application and related services.

1. About AppTesting

AppTesting is a platform that helps app developers and testers participate in organized application testing activities. Users may participate as testers, developers, or both.

AppTesting may provide testing commitments, quick tests, testing progress, feedback features, notifications, and related functionality.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use AppTesting, we may collect:

  • Google account information used for authentication, such as your name, email address, and Google account identifier.
  • Your AppTesting user account identifier.
  • Information about applications you add to AppTesting.
  • Testing commitment and participation information.
  • Testing check-ins and testing progress.
  • Feedback, ratings, comments, and bug reports submitted through the application.
  • Testing Coins and wallet transaction information.
  • Group membership and testing assignment information.
  • Information required to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

3. Google Sign-In

AppTesting uses Google Sign-In for account authentication. When you sign in with Google, AppTesting may receive basic account information provided by Google, such as your name, email address, and Google account identifier.

We use this information to create and maintain your AppTesting account, authenticate you, and provide AppTesting services.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected by AppTesting may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Provide testing and developer functionality.
  • Manage testing commitments and assignments.
  • Record testing participation and progress.
  • Manage Testing Coins and wallet transactions.
  • Provide feedback and bug-reporting functionality.
  • Display appropriate account and testing information.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, duplicate participation, and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve the service.
  • Communicate important service-related information.

5. Testing Information

When you participate in a testing commitment, AppTesting may record information such as your testing assignment, testing days, check-ins, commitment status, completion status, and related server-generated progress information.

This information is used to administer testing commitments and determine whether the requirements of a testing cycle have been met.

6. Feedback and Anonymity

Testers may submit ratings, comments, and bug reports for applications they are eligible to review.

Where AppTesting displays tester feedback to a developer, the feedback is designed to be presented without directly identifying the tester to the developer.

AppTesting may retain internal information necessary to enforce eligibility, prevent duplicate submissions, and maintain the integrity of the feedback system.

7. Testing Coins

AppTesting may use Testing Coins as an internal participation mechanism. Testing Coins are not cash, are not intended to represent currency, and cannot be withdrawn as money.

Wallet balances and transactions may be recorded to maintain an accurate server-authoritative transaction history.

8. Firebase and Cloud Services

AppTesting uses Firebase and related Google Cloud services to provide authentication, database, server-side functionality, and other application infrastructure.

Information may be processed and stored through these services as necessary to operate AppTesting.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, and users should understand that no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Data Sharing

AppTesting does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers and infrastructure providers that help operate AppTesting, including authentication, database, hosting, analytics, security, and other technical services.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, enforce security, investigate abuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

11. Developer and Tester Information

AppTesting may show limited information to other users when necessary for testing functionality.

Developers may receive information related to testing progress and feedback required to operate their testing activities. Sensitive account information is not intentionally exposed through ordinary testing workflows.

12. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide AppTesting, maintain account and transaction records, enforce testing rules, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

13. Account and Data Deletion

If you want to request deletion of your AppTesting account or personal information, you may contact the AppTesting service through the available support or contact channel associated with the application.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, financial or transaction records, dispute resolution, or enforcement of legitimate service requirements.

14. Children's Privacy

AppTesting is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law to independently use the service.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

15. Third-Party Services

AppTesting may rely on third-party services to provide authentication, infrastructure, hosting, notifications, analytics, security, or other technical functionality.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in AppTesting, applicable law, or our practices.

When material changes are made, the updated policy will be published at this location with a revised effective or update date.

17. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by AppTesting, please use the support or contact method provided with the AppTesting application or service.
